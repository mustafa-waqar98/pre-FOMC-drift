\section{Data}
The outcome is the log return from the prior day's 16:00 price to the 13:45 price on the meeting day, net of the risk-free rate, in basis points, on intraday S\&P~500 prices. The state variable is the VIX level five trading days before the meeting. The development sample covers 77 scheduled meetings in 2010--2019 and 2{,}239 non-meeting days. The holdout covers 2020--2026: 53 scheduled meetings, of which 52 pass the validity filter and one is dropped. Half-day sessions and days with missing trades are flagged and excluded by the panel builder, which logs row counts at every merge. The anxious/calm threshold is the development-sample median VIX, 15.07, fixed before the holdout was touched. The press-conference indicator is known only from 2021, so the 2020 meetings are excluded from that subsample (status UNVERIFIED for 2020).
