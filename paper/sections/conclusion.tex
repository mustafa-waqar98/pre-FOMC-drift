\section{Conclusion}
A single pre-specified holdout run finds a mean pre-announcement excess return of 38.3~bps in 2020--2026 that exceeds matched non-meeting days by 55.5~bps, but it does not confirm that the premium is concentrated in high-uncertainty meetings: the anxious-minus-calm contrast is $-2.6$~bps against $+19.2$~bps in development. Power is limited by seven year clusters and a calm cell of nine meetings. Verifying release times against primary sources also corrected the development benchmark, which now shows a small premium (2.8~bps) and no post-2015 decline. Natural next steps are per-meeting release times for the whole 1994--2019 history, intraday data before 2010, and a larger post-2020 sample as meetings accumulate.
